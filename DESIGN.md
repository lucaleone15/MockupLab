# MockupLab — design system

The rules every screen, picture and post follows. The values live in code: colours, fonts and easings in
[`src/index.css`](src/index.css) (`:root` tokens and the Tailwind `@theme`), the logo in
[`src/shell/Logo.tsx`](src/shell/Logo.tsx), the app icons in [`scripts/make-icons.mjs`](scripts/make-icons.mjs).
Change them there, not by hand in a component.

The browsable design system (tokens, live components, logo and pictures) is published at
https://claude.ai/artifact/K2w4F66eZ3fvdpWN7dArTS (private until shared). Its component files are rebuilt from
this code with `npm run build && npm run design-system -- <folder>/project`.

## The idea

**A photo studio for your designs.** The product is the picture you export: the interface stays quiet, light
and precise, like studio equipment. Freedom (you compose the scene as you like) and immediacy (the render is
live) come first. Nothing in the UI or the visuals competes with the user's design.

## Logo

- **The mark**: an upright phone in the brand blue, a lighter copy of it behind (35–42 % opacity) for depth —
  the « 3D » — and a white island. Never tilted, never outlined, never in another colour than the brand blue
  (on a coloured background, put it on the graphite tile).
- **The wordmark**: « MockupLab », one word, Gabarito 700, the same colour as the text around it
  (`#16171b` on light, white on dark). Mark and wordmark side by side, gap ≈ 0.35× the mark's height.
- **Clear space**: half the mark's width on every side. **Minimum size**: 16 px for the mark alone, 24 px
  with the wordmark.
- **App icon**: the mark on a graphite tile (`#3a3b42 → #111113`, light from the top left), macOS grid
  (824/1024, radius 185). Generated by `node scripts/make-icons.mjs`.

## Colour

| Token | Light | Dark | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| `brand-solid` | `#2f6bff` | `#2f6bff` | Filled buttons, the mark, active dots. White text on it: 4.5:1 (AA). |
| `brand-solid-hover` | `#2a62f0` | `#2a62f0` | Hover of the above. |
| `brand` | `#2f6bff` | `#4b8bff` | Blue text and icons (lighter on dark backgrounds to stay readable). |
| `accent` | `#2f6bff` | `#f5f5f7` | The app's primary action (blue on light, white on dark). |
| `fg` / `fg-muted` / `fg-subtle` | `#16171b` / `#5c5d66` / `#5f606a` | `#f3f3f6` / `#a2a3aa` / `#999aa2` | Text, by importance. |
| `bg`, `surface…`, `line…` | see `index.css` | | Frosted panels over a soft lit field. |
| `success` / `danger` | `#1f9d55` / `#d70015` | | States only. |

**One blue.** No other hue in the interface; colour comes from the user's designs and backgrounds.
Shadows are tinted night-blue (`rgb(22 32 72 / …)`), never plain grey.

## Type

- **Titles**: Gabarito (variable), always weight 700, letter-spacing -0.025em (class `font-display`). Tight leading
  (0.96–1.04), `text-balance`. Size makes the hierarchy, not weight.
- **Text**: Plus Jakarta Sans (variable). App: 13 px base, 15–17 px on the landing; leading 1.45–1.6.
- Fonts inside renders (brand kits, device screens, the client board: Geist, Archivo, Instrument Serif and imported
  fonts) belong to the content, never to the interface.

## Shape, depth, motion

- **Radius**: 10 px controls, 18–22 px cards and pictures, full pills for floating bars and buttons.
- **Depth**: three levels — panels, menus and windows, floating bars — as frosted glass (`--glass-*`) with
  one light from above.
- **Motion**: strong ease-out (`--ease-out`) for what enters or answers (160–340 ms), ease-in-out for what
  moves on screen; keyboard actions never animate; everything respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Phones

The whole editor works by finger below 768 px: the top bar keeps the mark, the mode as a menu, undo, ⋯ and an
export icon; the Studio's pages become a tab bar at the bottom with a folding panel above it; Rapide and Projet
are one scrolling column. On touch screens, what a computer shows on hover is shown outright, and « Choisir une
image » opens the system picker. Checked by `node scripts/check-mobile.mjs`.

## Pictures (landing, README, posts)

- **Real renders only**, made by MockupLab itself (`node scripts/make-showcase.mjs`), with the bundled sample
  designs.
- **Clean studio sets**: a seamless background — deep night (`#1d2340 → #07080c`) or a soft cool tint
  (`#e7eaf2`, `#eceff5`) — studio light, at most a plain painted podium. **No busy materials** in what we put
  forward: no terrazzo, wood grain, marble or plant shadows (they exist in the app for users who want them).
- The device and the design are the subject: centred, with air around them, no props.
- Link previews: 1200×630, night background, the mark, one tagline (`node scripts/make-og.mjs`).

## Voice

- French first, « tu », short sentences, no jargon (« mock-up », « Studio », « Rapide », « Projet »).
  English: « you », same tone.
- Say what it does, not how clever it is: « Glisse une capture, obtiens un mock-up photoréaliste en 4K. »
- Promises we keep: free, no account, nothing leaves your computer. The story is about the pictures, not the code: no « open source » or GitHub in headlines.
